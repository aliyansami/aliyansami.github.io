Privacy Policy

Aliyan Sami, freelance web developer · Effective 11 November 2026 · Contact: itsaliyansami@gmail.com

This page explains how I handle data on this website and in "n8n outreach", a private automation I use to run my own email outreach. The automation is used only by me, on my own computer, with my own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

This website

This portfolio site has no sign-up, no forms and no tracking or advertising cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Google data the automation accesses

With my permission, the automation connects to my own Google account (itsaliyansami@gmail.com) and uses:

  • Gmail: to send my outreach emails and follow-ups from my account, and to read replies and delivery-failure notices in my inbox so I know who replied or unsubscribed.
  • Google Sheets and Drive: to read and update my own lead-tracking spreadsheet.

It only ever accesses my own account. It does not access anyone else's Google account or data.

How that data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to manage my own outreach: tracking who I emailed, who replied and when to follow up.
  • It stays in my Google account and in the automation running on my own computer. It is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising.
  • The only other service involved is an optional notification to my own phone (via Telegram) when someone replies.
  • The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Businesses I contact

I contact businesses using contact details they publish themselves (for example on their own website or public maps). I store only business information: the business name, website, public email and phone number, and notes about our conversation. Every email includes a way to opt out: reply "no thanks" and I won't email you again. To have your details removed, email itsaliyansami@gmail.com and I will delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date above.
