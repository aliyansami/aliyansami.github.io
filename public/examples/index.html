What changes in every redesign

  1. Works on every phoneMost visitors arrive on a phone. The layout, text and buttons fit any screen with no pinching or zooming.
  2. Online booking or orderingCustomers book or buy in a minute, any time of day, instead of calling during opening hours.
  3. Clear calls to action"Book", "Call" and "Shop" buttons are always in reach, so visitors know exactly what to do next.
  4. Fast to loadLightweight pages with no heavy images or plugins, so they open quickly even on mobile data.
  5. Secure HTTPSNo "Not secure" warning in the browser, and forms that customers can trust.
  6. Easy to find and trustHours, location, services and Google reviews up front, set up so locals can find you when they search.
