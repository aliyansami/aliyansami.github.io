Welcome!

Welcome to the Kestrel Vale Veterinary Clinic website. We are a small animal practice providing medical, surgical and dental care for dogs, cats, rabbits and other small pets. Our caring vets and nurses have been looking after the pets of our community for many years and we treat every animal as if it were our own.

Appointments are available Monday to Saturday. To book an appointment please phone the clinic. Our phone lines can be busy in the mornings so please be patient. Please arrive 10 minutes early for your appointment and bring your pet's vaccination card.
